01

Getting started

Four steps from sign-up to your first order. Most vendors are fully set up in under a day.

1

Create your vendor account

Download the GoBuyMe Vendor app or sign up at [SIGNUP URL]. You'll verify your phone number with a one-time code.

2

Complete your business profile

Add your business name, category, opening hours, cover photo and logo, then choose your commission tier and build your starting menu.

3

Submit verification documents

Upload the ID and business documents listed under What you'll need for our team to review.

4

Go live

Your dashboard is open right away, so you can finish your menu while verification is in progress — a status banner shows your approval progress. Once approved, toggle your store open and orders start coming in.

02

What you'll need

Have these ready before you start — it makes the profile step much faster.

Valid ID
National ID, driver's licence, voter's card or international passport
Business documents
CAC registration or relevant trade licence, where applicable
Bank account details
Account name, number and bank — this is where payouts are sent
Photos
A storefront/cover photo, a logo, and clear photos of your menu items
03

Approval & verification

Once your documents are submitted, our operations team reviews them for approval. You don't need to wait idle — your dashboard and menu builder are usable immediately, so you can get everything ready while review is underway.

A banner on your dashboard shows where you are: Pending review, Changes needed, or Approved. You can start accepting live orders only once your status reaches Approved.

Tip: Clear, well-lit photos of your ID and documents are the most common reason approval is delayed. Avoid glare and cropped edges.
04

Choose your commission tier

You pick a tier when you set up your profile, and can switch later from your dashboard settings.

Tier 1
3% commission

The lowest-cost way to get started. Your store appears in normal search and category browsing.

  • No promotion card placement
  • Standard listing visibility
Tier 2
7.5% commission

A higher commission unlocks promoted placement to help new and repeat customers find you first.

  • Promotion cards on the customer home screen
  • One active promotion at a time
Switching from Tier 2 down to Tier 1 automatically deactivates any active promotion cards.
05

Menu & photos

Add items with a name, description, price and photo from the Menu tab. You can update prices, mark items sold out, or remove items any time — changes reflect for customers immediately.

Listings with real photos of the actual dish or product consistently get more orders than listings using placeholder or stock images.

06

Promotions — Tier 2

Tier 2 vendors can create a promotion card that appears in the customer app's carousel. Only one promotion can be active at a time, so make it count.

Image size — 1080 × 580 px
Aspect ratio — 1.86 : 1
Safe zone — 60 px margin for text/logo
07

Orders & delivery

New orders arrive on your dashboard in real time. Accept or reject within the order window, then mark ready when it's time for a rider to collect.

Delivery is handled entirely by GoBuyMe riders — you're never asked to arrange your own delivery. The fee shown to the customer is calculated by distance from your store:

ComponentAmount
Base fee₦500
Per kilometre₦100
Maximum fee₦3,000
08

Payouts

Your earnings from completed orders, minus commission, are settled to the bank account on file automatically — there's no invoicing and nothing to request.

Payouts run in a daily batch each morning. You can track individual earnings and past payouts from the Earnings tab on your dashboard at any time.
